How Google ranking has evolved

Old method: count the votes

A site with more votes or a higher frequency of mentions ranks higher. It is simple, but easy to game: anyone can pile up votes without earning trust.

New method: follow the links

A site earns authority from the quality and quantity of links pointing to it. A link from an important page is worth more, and a page that links out to many sites passes on a smaller share to each.

Formula used here

PR(A) = (1 − d) + d × Σ( PR(T) / C(T) ) with d = 0.85. PR(T) is the score of a page T linking to A, and C(T) is how many links T sends out. The scores start at 1 and repeat until they change by less than 0.000001 (or 100 rounds).
